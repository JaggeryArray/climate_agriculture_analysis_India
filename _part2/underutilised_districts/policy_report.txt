UNDERUTILISED DISTRICTS — POLICY REPORT
Identifying districts with no crop data but high model-predicted yield potential
======================================================================

METHODOLOGY
----------------------------------------
  Three models were trained per crop on existing district data (KNN, Random Forest, XGBoost).
  Each model predicts the yield class for districts that have climate data but NO historical
  crop records. A district × crop pair is flagged as 'High Potential' when at least
  2 out of 3 models predict the 'High' yield class.
  The ensemble probability is the average of all three models' P(High).

POLICY RECOMMENDATIONS
----------------------------------------
  1. PRIORITY SOIL SURVEYS: Dispatch agronomy teams to the top-ranked districts
     to verify climate-model assumptions with ground-truth soil and irrigation audits.

  2. PILOT PROGRAMMES: Launch small-scale cultivation trials in the top 10 districts
     for the crop(s) with highest ensemble P(High), using local farmer cooperatives.

  3. DATA COLLECTION IMPERATIVE: All flagged districts currently lack official crop
     yield records. Establishing monitoring points here will close the data gap and
     allow future model retraining with observed outcomes.

  4. INFRASTRUCTURE ALIGNMENT: Cross-reference high-potential districts with existing
     irrigation, road, and market-access databases to prioritise those where investment
     marginal cost is lowest.

  5. CAVEATS: Model predictions are based on climate features alone. Soil quality,
     water availability, socio-economic conditions, and local agronomic expertise
     must be considered before any policy commitment.
